# Milestone 1 — Thermal Printer Setup Guide

---

## Bluetooth Thermal Printer Setup (Android Reception App)

1. **Pair Thermal Printer in Android Settings**:
   - Turn on Bluetooth thermal printer.
   - Open Android Device Settings $\rightarrow$ Bluetooth $\rightarrow$ Pair New Device.
   - Select your printer (e.g. `POS-58` or `POS-80`) and enter PIN (usually `0000` or `1234`).

2. **Configure Printer in Reception App**:
   - Open Reception App $\rightarrow$ Settings $\rightarrow$ **Printer Settings**.
   - Select paper width: **58mm** or **80mm**.
   - Toggle **Auto-Print on Token Generation**.
   - Click **Send Test Receipt Print**.
